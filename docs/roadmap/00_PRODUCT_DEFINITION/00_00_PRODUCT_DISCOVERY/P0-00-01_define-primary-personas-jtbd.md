# Primary Personas & Jobs To Be Done (JTBD)

## 1. "The Signal Hunter" (Primary)
**Who:** Amateur radio operator, SWL (Shortwave Listener), or spectrum enthusiast.
**Context:** Sits at a desk with a HackRF/RTL-SDR, scanning for interesting signals.
**Jobs:**
- "I want to visualize the spectrum to find active frequencies."
- "I want to tune quickly to a signal I see."
- "I want to identify unknown signals (Is this LoRa? P25? FM?)."
**Frustrations:**
- Native apps (SDR#, SDR++) require complex driver installs.
- Web apps usually lack waterfall resolution or audio quality.

## 2. "The Field Engineer" (Secondary)
**Who:** Professional RF engineer or technician debugging a system.
**Context:** On a laptop in a server room or field site.
**Jobs:**
- "I need to check if this transmitter is actually outputting power."
- "I need to record a sample of this interference to analyze later."
**Frustrations:**
- Can't install unapproved software on work laptop.
- Needs to share recordings with colleagues easily.

## 3. "The Learner" (Tertiary)
**Who:** Student or hobbyist learning about DSP/Radio.
**Context:** No hardware, just a browser.
**Jobs:**
- "I want to understand what 'IQ' data looks like."
- "I want to play back a recording to see how filters work."
